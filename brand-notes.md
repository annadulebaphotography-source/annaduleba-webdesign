## How this should translate into the website

Anna does not just build websites. She collects scattered ideas, sees patterns, finds what is unclear or broken, and turns it into a coherent brand experience.

Core message:

Website, branding, visuals and client communication should feel like one clear system.

Client should feel:

- She understands my business deeply.
- She can organize my ideas and make them clear.
- She sees what is missing in my communication.
- She can turn my brand, website and message into one coherent whole.
- My website will not only look beautiful, it will explain, guide and sell.

Possible German copy direction:

"Ich erkenne schnell, was in einem Business schon da ist, was noch unklar wirkt und welche Verbindung zwischen Angebot, Marke und Kommunikation fehlt. Daraus entsteht eine Website, die nicht wie ein einzelnes Designprojekt wirkt, sondern wie ein stimmiger Ausdruck deines gesamten Business."

Avoid:

- Listing Gallup strengths directly on the homepage.
- Making it sound like a CV or personality test.

Use:

- In "Über mich"
- In "So arbeite ich"
- In a subtle paragraph about method, clarity and brand coherence.
